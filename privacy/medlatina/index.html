PRIVACY POLICY

Medlatina

PACKAGE
cz.medlatina.app
EFFECTIVE
On release (version 1.0)
CONTACT
onskteam@gmail.com

Medlatina works offline, without an account, ads or analytics. Your learning progress stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

The app itself is in Czech; menu names on this page are translated into English.

Who runs the app

Operator: Wickroot. Privacy questions: onskteam@gmail.com.

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • Learning progress: finished lessons, the review schedule, XP, streak, daily goal, the faculty you chose and your exam date.
  • Settings and rewards: reminder time, sounds on/off, Ossík's outfit, the colour theme, chapters unlocked as streak rewards, and the nickname you may enter as a supporter.
  • Purchases: whether you have the full course and how many coffees you bought for Ossík (to unlock the rewards).

All of this is kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the app does not do

Medlatina has no server and does not send your progress anywhere. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools and does not collect device identifiers.

Purchases

The full course (a one-time purchase) and the voluntary "Buy Ossík a coffee" tips are sold and processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment details; the app only gets a confirmation from the store that a purchase is valid.

Reporting a mistake in the content

The "Report a mistake" button opens your e-mail app with a pre-filled message (the item and the content version). Sending it is up to you. If you send it, we will see your e-mail address and your message and use them only to fix the content and reply to you.

Permissions

  • Notifications – only for the daily reminder, if you turn it on. The reminder is scheduled on your phone and does not use the internet.
  • Run after restart – so that a scheduled reminder keeps working after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play Billing – only for purchases, through the Google Play Store app.

Children

The app is meant for university students and is not directed at children.

Your rights

We do not store anything about you on servers, so we have no data to provide or delete – it is all on your phone. If you e-mailed us, you can ask us to delete that e-mail. You can also file a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.gov.cz).

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if syncing between devices is added), the new version will be published at this address and mentioned in the app update notes.
