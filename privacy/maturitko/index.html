PRIVACY POLICY

Maturitko

PACKAGE
cz.maturitko.app
EFFECTIVE
1 October 2026 (version 1.0)
CONTACT
onskteam@gmail.com

In short

Maturitko works without an account and without an internet connection. Everything you do or write in the app stays on your device.

The operator has no access to this data – it is never sent anywhere, sold or shared. The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who runs the app

Operator: Wickroot.

Privacy questions: onskteam@gmail.com.

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • Learning progress: completed lessons, best score, number of attempts, XP earned and the days you studied (streak).
  • Weak spots: exercises you got wrong and when to review them.
  • Test results: score, time and date of each attempt.
  • Literature and essays: your list of 20 books, notes on books, essay drafts and ticked criteria.
  • Settings: exam year and date, second exam subject, selected subject, sounds, reminder time and the limit of books per author.

The app does not know your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and it does not ask for these permissions.

Please don't write sensitive personal data (for example about your health) into notes or essays – they stay on the device, but they are also part of backups.

What the data is used for

Only to make the app work: to remember your progress, offer your mistakes for review, count down to the exam and check the rules of your book list. There is no profiling and no automated decision-making.

Where the data is stored and for how long

In the app's private storage on your phone (in the web version, in the browser's storage). Other apps cannot access it.

The data stays until you delete it: in the app under Profil → Nastavení → "Smazat všechna data" (Profile → Settings → Delete all data), by uninstalling the app, or (on the web) by clearing the site data in your browser.

Backups

Manual backup: Nastavení → "Uložit zálohu" (Settings → Save backup) creates a file in a place you choose. The file is not encrypted – who you share it with is up to you. You restore it with Nastavení → "Obnovit ze zálohy" (Settings → Restore from backup).

Android system backup: if Google backup is turned on in your phone, Android may save the app's data (up to 25 MB) to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. On Android 9 and newer this backup is encrypted with your screen lock and the operator cannot read it. It follows your phone settings and Google's terms; you can turn it off in your phone settings (Google → Backup).

Permissions

Notifications: the app asks for this only when you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone; it does not use the internet.

Run after restart: so that a scheduled reminder keeps working after the phone restarts.

File picker: you save and open backups through the system dialog; the app cannot see your other files.

Sharing data

With no one. The app does not communicate with any server. All content (exercises, recordings, fonts) is part of the app and nothing is downloaded.

Children and teenagers

The app is meant for secondary school students preparing for the Czech maturita exam. Because it collects no personal data, it does not require parental consent.

Your rights

You are fully in control of your data: you can see it in the app, export it with a backup and delete it with one button. The operator does not have your data and therefore cannot provide, correct or delete it for you.

If you believe your personal data is processed in breach of the law (GDPR), you can file a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.gov.cz).

Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device. We recommend using a screen lock and saving backups only to places you trust.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using online services in the future (for example an account, sync, paid features or crash reporting), we will update this policy in advance, tell you about the change in the app and only then start processing new data – where required, only with your consent.

The current version is always in the app (Profil → Nastavení → O aplikaci, i.e. Profile → Settings → About) and on the page linked from the Google Play listing.
