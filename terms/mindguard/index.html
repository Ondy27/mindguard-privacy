About the app

MindGuard helps you quit adult content: it blocks adult websites in supported web browsers and offers a clean-days counter, breathing and meditation exercises and a panic button. Operator: Wickroot, contact: onskteam@gmail.com. By using the app you agree to these terms; how the app handles data is described in the Privacy policy. The app is intended for adults (18+).

Not medical treatment

MindGuard is a self-help tool. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional help. If you feel that you cannot cope, or you are in crisis, please contact a doctor, a therapist or a crisis line.

How blocking works and its limits

Blocking uses the Android Accessibility Service, only after you agree to it in the app, and works in supported browsers. No filter is perfect: some pages may not be blocked and others may be blocked by mistake. You can turn the service off at any time in Android settings.

MindGuard Pro

Pro features are sold through Google Play as subscriptions or a lifetime purchase. The price and the billing period are shown in Google Play before you pay. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions). Refunds are handled by Google Play under its rules.
