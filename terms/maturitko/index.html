About the app

Maturitko is an educational app for preparing for the Czech maturita exam. Operator: Wickroot, contact: onskteam@gmail.com.

By using the app you agree to these terms. How the app handles data is described in the Privacy policy.

The app itself is in Czech; menu names on this page are translated into English.

Content and its accuracy

Exercises, summaries and book summaries are carefully prepared and checked, but they may still contain mistakes. The app supplements teaching – it does not replace school, textbooks or official materials.

The operator is not responsible for the result of your maturita exam. If you find a mistake, please let us know at the contact above.

Not an official app

Maturitko is not a product of CERMAT or the Czech Ministry of Education (MŠMT) and is not affiliated with them in any way. The practice tests are our own and only imitate the format of the exam. Always check dates, rules and requirement catalogues at maturita.cermat.cz and at your school.

Each school sets its own topics for the profile (school) part of the exam – the topics in the app are a general foundation.

Copyright

Exercise texts, summaries, book summaries, the mascot, sounds and graphics are the operator's copyrighted works. You may use them freely for your own study; you may not copy them in bulk, distribute them or use them commercially without permission.

The app also includes third-party works under open licences (fonts, listening voices, libraries) – they are listed in the app under Settings → Licences.

Your data and backups

Your notes and essays belong to you. Data is stored only on your device: if you lose your phone or uninstall the app without a backup, the data is lost. So save a backup now and then (Settings → Save backup).
